Job Title: Data Scientist

Job Summary:
We are looking for a Data Scientist with 3+ years of experience to join our analytics team.
The ideal candidate will build predictive models, analyze large datasets, and communicate
insights to business stakeholders.

Responsibilities:
- Analyze large structured and unstructured datasets to extract actionable insights
- Build, validate and deploy machine learning models
- Perform feature engineering and statistical analysis
- Collaborate with engineering teams to productionize models
- Present findings to non-technical stakeholders

Requirements:
- 3+ years of experience in data science or machine learning
- Strong proficiency in Python, SQL
- Hands-on experience with pandas, numpy, scikit-learn
- Experience with machine learning, deep learning and NLP
- Familiarity with TensorFlow or PyTorch is a plus
- Experience with AWS or Azure cloud platforms
- Experience building and consuming REST APIs using FastAPI
- Knowledge of Docker for containerization
- Experience with Git for version control
- Strong communication and problem-solving skills
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Statistics or a related field

Nice to have:
- Experience with Power BI or Tableau for data visualization
- Experience with PySpark for big data processing
- Knowledge of Scikit-learn Pipelines
