Job Title: Data Engineer

Job Summary:
We are seeking a Data Engineer with 4+ years of experience to design and maintain
scalable data pipelines.

Responsibilities:
- Design, build and maintain ETL/ELT pipelines
- Optimize SQL queries and database performance
- Work with PySpark for large-scale data processing
- Deploy data pipelines on AWS or GCP
- Ensure data quality and reliability

Requirements:
- 4+ years of experience in data engineering
- Strong proficiency in Python and SQL
- Hands-on experience with PySpark and Spark
- Experience with AWS, GCP or Azure
- Experience with Docker and CI/CD pipelines
- Familiarity with Git and version control
- Knowledge of data warehousing concepts
- Experience with Airflow or similar orchestration tools is a plus

Nice to have:
- Experience with Power BI or Tableau
- Exposure to machine learning workflows
- Knowledge of FastAPI for building internal tools
