AI/ML Engineer Python, LLM & Agentic AI

Role Summary
We are looking for a practical AI/ML Engineer with strong Python development capabilities to build and maintain AI-enabled, high-reliability backend and data-driven systems within a regulated financial market / stock exchange environment.
The role focuses on implementing real-world AI solutions using Python, Large Language Models (LLMs), APIs, prompt engineering, AI development tools, Model Context Protocol (MCP), tool creation, and agentic workflows. The emphasis is on hands-on implementation and problem-solving rather than purely traditional Machine Learning or theoretical model development.
The engineer will work on secure, performant, and mission-critical platforms where correctness, auditability, stability, and responsible use of AI are essential.

Key Responsibilities
•     Design, develop, and maintain backend services, APIs, and AI-enabled applications using Python.
•     Build practical AI systems using LLMs, prompt engineering, tools, APIs, and agentic workflows.
•     Design and implement AI agents capable of using tools, APIs, data sources, and structured workflows to complete business tasks.
•     Create reusable tools and integrations that can be consumed by AI agents and internal applications.
•     Implement and manage Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers, clients, or similar AI orchestration and tool-integration mechanisms.
•     Build and manage integrations with internal and external third-party systems.
•     Work with locally hosted or offline AI models, including model deployment, inference, configuration, and performance considerations.
•     Apply AI-assisted or vibe coding development practices responsibly to improve productivity, prototyping speed, testing, documentation, and code quality.
•     Work on data-intensive and event-driven components supporting market systems.
•     Implement and maintain CI/CD pipelines for controlled and auditable deployments.
•     Contribute to system architecture with a focus on security, reliability, scalability, and performance.
•     Ensure that AI-generated outputs and automated workflows are appropriately validated, logged, monitored, and auditable.
•     Ensure compliance with internal governance, security, audit, and regulatory standards.
•     Participate in code reviews, testing, documentation, deployment, and operational-readiness activities.
